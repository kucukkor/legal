# Penguin Showdown Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026

Penguin Showdown is published by the game's independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. Contact: <info@esimostudios.com>.

## Summary

- The game has no accounts and no server of its own. Your progress is saved on your device.
- We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts or precise location.
- The game uses two third-party services: **RevenueCat** for purchases and **Google AdMob** for ads.

## Your progress

Your progress, your settings and the purchases already added to your game are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them from your device.

## Purchases: RevenueCat

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat verifies your purchases so the game can deliver and restore them. The game connects to RevenueCat with an anonymous ID and does not send your name, e-mail address or advertising identifier. For this, RevenueCat collects your purchase history.

- RevenueCat privacy policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/>

## Ads: Google AdMob

The game shows full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that play only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects device or other identifiers (including the advertising identifier), approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostics. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. On iOS, the game asks for permission to track; if you decline, ads are not personalized. **Remove Ads** turns off the ads between levels.

- Google ads privacy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

The game uses no analytics service of its own.

## Protection and retention

Data is sent encrypted. RevenueCat and Google protect the data they receive at least as well as this policy describes, under their own policies. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as your purchases and the law require; advertising data is kept by Google under its policy.

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your choices

- **Ads:** limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
- **Purchase records:** e-mail us the order number from your store receipt and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them.
- **Your progress:** delete the game.

## Changes and contact

If an update adds a service that handles data, we will update this policy before it is released. Questions: <info@esimostudios.com>.
