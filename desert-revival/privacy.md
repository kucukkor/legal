# Privacy Policy for Desert Revival

**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026

This policy explains what data **Desert Revival** ("the game") handles and why. Desert Revival is published by the game's independent developer ("we"). You can reach us at <info@esimostudios.com>.

## 1. The short version

- No accounts, no sign-up and no game server: your oasis is saved on your device.
- The game does not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
- Two third-party services are used: **RevenueCat** for purchases and the Caravan Pass subscription, and **Google AdMob** for ads.
- **No Ads**, the **Starter Pack** (which includes No Ads) and an active **Caravan Pass** turn interstitial ads off.

## 2. Data on your device

Your progress, the purchases already added to your save, your settings and your language are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them from your device.

## 3. RevenueCat: purchases

Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat checks the store receipt so the game can deliver your purchases, keep the Caravan Pass active and restore your purchases later. The game uses RevenueCat with an anonymous ID created on your device, without your name, e-mail address or advertising identifier. RevenueCat also gives us purchase statistics that do not identify you.

- RevenueCat privacy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/>

## 4. Google AdMob: ads

The game shows an occasional full-screen ad between moments of play, never during the tutorial, and optional rewarded ads that play only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, AdMob processes your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers, technical information about your device, your approximate location (estimated from your IP address), how you interact with ads, and diagnostic and crash information. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

- **On iOS,** the game asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, ads are not personalized.
- **No Ads** and an active **Caravan Pass** turn interstitial ads off. Rewarded ads stay available, because they only play when you ask.

We add no analytics of our own: no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry or similar service.

- Google ads privacy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

## 5. Protection and retention

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). RevenueCat and Google protect your data at least as well as this policy describes, under their own policies. Your save stays on your device until you delete the game. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as your purchases need them and the law requires. Advertising data is kept by Google under its policy.

## 6. What the game does not use

No social logins, no cloud save, no push notifications, and no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location. If a future update adds a service that handles data, this policy will be updated before that update ships.

## 7. Children

Desert Revival is intended for players aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a child made a purchase without permission, ask the App Store or Google Play for a refund, and write to us.

## 8. Deleting your data

- **Your save:** delete the game.
- **Purchase records:** e-mail us and we will file a deletion request with RevenueCat on your behalf.
- **Ad data:** reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings (iOS: Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Google → Ads).

## 9. Contact

<info@esimostudios.com>. We answer within a few business days.
